The deacons are ordained to do their work according to Acts 6:1-8, 1 Timothy 3:8-13 and Titus 2.

The deacons assist the pastor, cooperate with the pastor in providing pulpit supply and leaders for prayer meetings, visit members, and care for the sick, needy, and distressed members of the church. The board promotes Christian instruction and ministry to the church membership.
