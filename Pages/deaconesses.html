The Board of Deaconesses are sisters in the church who are moral, spiritual, and of good report among the members as suggested in the New Testament: 1 Timothy 3:11 and Titus 2:2-5.

Their duties include assisting the deacons in maintaining moral and spiritual life among members and assisting the pastor in developing the spiritual life of women and girls for Christian service. The board also assists in visiting and caring for sick, needy, and distressed church members.
