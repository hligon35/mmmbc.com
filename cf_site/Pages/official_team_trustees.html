The Official Team is responsible for fiscal policy, assists the pastor in fulfilling the church program, hires and terminates employees of the church (pastor excluded), and helps determine the use of church and fellowship or educational facilities for extra religious activities.

The Trustee Board is responsible for church property and takes necessary measures for its protection and management.
